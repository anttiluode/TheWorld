## Why Sigh-image changed the next gate

```text

 -> checker with image-dependent modulation
 -> bent / moire-like interference
 -> coarser curved structure

```

That does not prove a brain code. It suggests a systems question:

> **Can visual innovations become local in space × scale × time rather than merely local in pixels?**

If yes, scale/frequency may supply a useful candidate-routing geometry for CC0.

