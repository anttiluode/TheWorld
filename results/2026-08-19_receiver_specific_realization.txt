Verdict
====================================================================
Same world, different receiver, different required dynamical degree.

This is standard linear-systems realization theory, not a new theorem. Its role in this repo is to replace the vague sentence "each receiver has a small piece of the world" with a measurable question:

  what is the smallest dynamical realization that preserves every consequence
  of the world that can reach this receiver?

For exact finite-dimensional LTI systems the Hankel rank gives the minimal realization order under the usual controllability/observability conditions. In learned/noisy systems use the Hankel singular-value spectrum / approximate degree rather than brittle exact rank.

Carry-forward test for WorldSplat / TinyAvatar:
- estimate empirical receiver-specific Hankel spectra;
- compare RGB, depth, motion, collision, pose, identity, etc.;
- if all spectra stay broad/full, the receiver-compression story loses;
- if some collapse sharply, compile those receivers into cheaper FLOW/JUMP blocks.
