IMPORTANT INSTRUMENT FLAW FOUND AFTER RUN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A3 normalized every spectral channel by that frame's own spatial q95.
That creates a hidden global dependency: a local event can change q95 and
therefore rescale every spatial cell in the channel. This can artificially
inflate spatial routing fanout. The tile edge channel used a similar framewise
normalization, though only for one of its two channels.

Therefore:
- the A3 run is preserved as a useful first receipt;
- the precise spectral-vs-tile routing fanout result is NOT final;
- A3B removes framewise global renormalization by freezing per-channel scales
  from the first frame and reports tile spatial fanout explicitly.
