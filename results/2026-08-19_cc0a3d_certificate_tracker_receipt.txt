INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Cross-scale multiplicity as a persistence signal survives a second real webcam run.
2. Naive spectral representation still does NOT win as a spatial router: the >=2-scale certificate touches ~2.28x as many current spatial cells as the plain tile event mask in this run.
3. The certificate tracker produced a modestly lower downstream WAKE fraction than the tile tracker (0.400 vs 0.438; about 8.7% relative reduction), with more REUSE and fewer UPDATE/REACQUIRE events.
4. That modest event saving came with materially larger belief fanout (~1.67x tile) and shorter mean track lifetime. Certificate tracking also had more create/expire churn.
5. Therefore A3D is a MIXED result, not an architecture win.
6. HOLD remains belief/state, not sensory support. The held-only footprint is explicitly billed.

CURRENT BEST INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not make the full Gabor field the runtime body.

The evidence points instead toward a separation:

  cheap spatial event stream  -> candidate ADDRESS / routing
  cross-scale agreement       -> persistence / validity CERTIFICATE
  local track state           -> persistent BELIEF / consequence
  consequence change          -> downstream WAKE

The spectral/multiscale machinery may be better used as a certificate, verifier, or teacher than as the primary routing representation.
